## Guardrails

- Do not force a setup label when the pattern is unclear.
- Do not treat a similar-looking chart as actionable without market context and risk location.
- Prefer "Watch" over "Actionable" when the entry is extended or the stop is too wide.
- Note when evidence is missing from the screenshot.
